Supported Surveys
=================
This page lists the surveys currently supported by ATK 'out of the box'.

Data Surveys
------------
- Any survey listed in Vizier (via its catalogue ID)
- Gaia
- Pan-STARRS
- SkyMapper
- GALEX
- ROSAT
- SDSS
- WISE
- 2MASS
- eROSITA

Reddening Surveys
-----------------
- STILISM
- GDRE

Light Curve Surveys
-------------------
- ZTF, bands: g, r, i
- ATLAS, bands: o, c, i
- Gaia, bands: g, bp, rp
- ASAS-SN (asassn), bands: g, v
- CRTS, bands: v
- TESS, bands: TESS mag

Image Surveys
-------------
- Pan-STARRS (panstarrs), bands = g, r, i, z, y
- SkyMapper, bands = g, r, i, z, u, v
- DSS, bands = g

HRD Surveys
-----------
- Gaia

Spectrum Surveys
----------------
- SDSS
